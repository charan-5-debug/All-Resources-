🚀 HTML: Your First Step into Web Development Welcome to the World of Web Development! Have you ever wondered how websites are created? How do websites display text, images, videos, buttons, and links? The answer begins with HTML — HyperText Markup Language.
HTML is the foundation of almost every website. It gives a webpage its structure and tells the browser what content to display. 🌟 Why Should You Learn HTML?
1. Build Your Own Websites Create personal websites, portfolios, educational websites, blogs, and landing pages.
2. Understand How Websites Work Learn how headings, paragraphs, images, links, lists, forms, and tables are displayed on a webpage.
3. Start Your Web Development Journey HTML is beginner-friendly and an excellent first step for students who want to enter the world of technology.
4. Create Real-World Projects Build a student portfolio, a free-learning resource hub, a college information page, or a scholarship information website.
5. Prepare for Advanced Technologies HTML provides the foundation for learning CSS, JavaScript, front-end frameworks, and full-stack development.
